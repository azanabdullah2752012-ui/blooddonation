Official Circular #AVM-2026/BD-01
Dated: September 2026

Dear Esteemed Residents & Friends of AVM Housing Society,

As Secretary of our vibrant housing society, it fills me with immense pride to announce our Annual Mega Blood Donation Drive scheduled for Friday, 30th October 2026 at our spacious City Hall.

"Blood is the most precious gift that anyone can give to another person — the gift of life. A decision to donate your blood can save a life, or even several if your blood is separated into its components."

This year, we have partnered with the premier medical team from the Red Cross Blood Transfusion Service and leading city hospital hematologists. Every single donation will directly support emergency trauma cases, children battling thalassemia, and oncology patients across nearby civil hospitals.

To ensure zero waiting time and unmatched convenience, we have divided the day into streamlined 45-minute slots between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM. Dedicated refreshment lounges with fresh juices, high-protein snacks, and doctor consultation zones are being arranged at City Hall.

I urge all residents across Wings A, B, C, D, E & F, along with our extended family members and guests, to register online today. Let us demonstrate the collective heart of AVM Housing Society!

Sunil
