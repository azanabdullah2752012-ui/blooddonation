An Appeal to Every Resident of AVM Housing Society

Dear Esteemed Residents, Neighbours, and Friends,

As Honorary Secretary of our residential community, I write to you with heartfelt conviction. On Friday, 30th October 2026, our society will transform the City Hall Ground Floor into a certified, life-giving blood donation sanctuary in formal association with the Indian Red Cross Society.

“Blood cannot be manufactured in a laboratory. It can only come as a voluntary gift of grace from one human heart to another. One morning of our time stands between a trauma patient or a young child with thalassemia and tomorrow.”

— Desk of Sunil, Honorary Secretary

We have taken meticulous clinical measures to ensure an effortless experience for every resident. The drive features seven 45-minute scheduled intervals between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM, eliminating queue times. Licensed hematologists will conduct complimentary vitals screenings, and our post-donation recovery lounge at City Hall patio is equipped with protein nutrition kits and fresh juices.

I urge all residents across Wings A, B, C, D, E, and F, as well as our extended families and workplace colleagues, to register your slot today. Let our society set the civic benchmark for compassion.

Sunil Honorary Secretary, AVM Housing Society

SAFETY PROTOCOLS

Our Fourfold Clinical Guarantee

Every procedure adheres to strict National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC) regulations.

01

100% Sterile Single-Use Needles

Unsealed only in direct visual presence of the donor, disposed of in biohazard sharps containers immediately after use.

02

Certified Hospital Phlebotomists

Administered exclusively by licensed doctors and certified hospital hematology nurses with over 10 years trauma experience.

03

Post-Donation Physician Lounge

Comfortable recliners, glucose hydration fluids, blood pressure recovery monitoring, and physician discharge sign-off.

04

Continuous Cold-Chain Transport

Collected blood is instantly transferred into 4°C pharmaceutical-grade mobile refrigeration units for hospital delivery.

Drink at least 2–3 tall glasses of water in the morning. Eat a nutritious, light breakfast or lunch at least 1 hour beforehand (never donate on an empty stomach). Avoid greasy or fried food on event day, and bring a government photo ID along with your digital or printed AVM Donor Pass.
